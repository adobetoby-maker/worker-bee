Welcome to the Worker Bee client experience. This is a short guide covering the things most clients ask about.

## Welcome

When Toby is building your website, he uses an internal system to track everything — your site status, any invoices, and any messages you've sent through your contact form. You don't need to log in anywhere. Toby shares specific links with you directly when there's something to see or do.

- Building — we're actively working on it

## Invoices

When it's time to invoice you, Toby will send you a link by text or email. That link opens a page showing:

- A list of everything covered (the work done, hosting, etc.)
- The total amount and due date
- A button to download or print the PDF for your records

You don't need an account to view it. Just open the link.

**If something looks wrong** — wrong amount, wrong name, a line item you don't recognize — text Toby right away. Invoices are easy to correct before payment.

## Contact Form Submissions

If your website has a contact form (most Worker Bee sites do), every submission is captured and logged. Toby can see exactly who filled out your form, what they wrote, and when they sent it.

This means:
- Nothing gets lost in a spam folder
- You can ask Toby "did anyone fill out the form this week?" and he'll know
- If there's a problem with the form (nobody submitting when they should be), Toby can check the logs

Right now, follow-up on those leads is your job — Toby captures them and you act on them. If you want automated email notifications when someone fills in the form, ask Toby and he can set that up.

## Updating Your Info

If any of the following changes, let Toby know:

- Phone number on your site
- Business hours
- Prices or service descriptions
- Address or service area
- Photos you want added or swapped

You don't edit the site yourself — just text or email Toby the update and he'll handle it, usually within 24 hours.
